Where it fits

Made for the doors other smart locks can't go on

Northern Door Lockworks installs INOX smart locks. The keypad and lever replace the handle set on the lock you already have, so the door keeps its multipoint or mortise lock and gains keyless entry.

  • Multipoint patio and entry doors. The tall locks that latch at the top, middle and bottom.
  • Mortise locks. The boxed lock body set into the edge of the door.
  • Narrow or unusual entrances. We measure your door before anything is ordered.
