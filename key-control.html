Interchangeable-core locks

Change a lock in minutes

In an interchangeable-core lock, the part the key goes into lifts out with a special control key. A new core drops in, and the old keys stop working. The lock itself never leaves the door.

  • Minutes, not hours. A door is rekeyed in the time it takes to swap the core.
  • Built for heavy use. Commercial-grade locks made for hotel rooms, offices and common doors.
  • Clean master keying. Each person opens their own door. Managers and maintenance carry one key that opens them all.
  • Spare cores on hand. Keyed and ready, so a problem door is fixed the same day.

When a key goes missing

With ordinary locks, a lost key means a service call and every lock on that key taken apart.

  • We swap the core at the affected door
  • The lost key is dead the moment the core comes out
  • Everyone else keeps the key they have
  • Your records are updated to match

Controlled keys

Keys that don't get copied behind your back

Most house and business keys can be copied in two minutes at any hardware store or kiosk. After a few years, nobody knows how many are out there. A controlled key ends that.

  • Not at the hardware store. The key blank isn't sold there, and kiosks can't copy it.
  • New keys come through us. Extra keys are cut only when the person you name approves.
  • Every key accounted for. A written record of who holds which key.

For homes and small properties

Schlage Everest cylinders go into standard deadbolts, so your hardware stays. The key can't be copied at a hardware store or kiosk.
